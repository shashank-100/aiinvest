## What Makes a Growth Stock?

A growth stock is a company expected to grow revenues and earnings significantly faster than the average company. Growth investors pay premium valuations today in exchange for the expectation of substantially higher earnings in the future.

The best growth stocks generate **compounding returns** — as the business grows and profits expand, the stock price follows.

## The Challenge of Growth Investing

Growth stocks are not cheap — by definition, they trade at premium valuations. You're paying for expected future growth. This creates two risks:

1. **The growth disappoints** — the stock collapses (see: any growth stock that missed estimates in 2022)
2. **Interest rates rise** — higher discount rates compress the present value of future earnings, crushing high-multiple stocks even if growth continues

Growth investing requires both identifying genuine growth and understanding the macro environment (interest rate direction).

## What AI Identifies in Quality Growth Stocks

AI-powered analysis separates the genuine growth compounders from the hyped story stocks by focusing on:

**Revenue growth consistency:** Is growth accelerating or decelerating? Decelerating growth at high multiples is dangerous.

**Gross margin expansion:** As a growth company scales, margins should expand. Flat or declining margins signal execution problems.

**Rule of 40:** For software companies, Revenue Growth Rate + Profit Margin should exceed 40. Companies above this threshold are efficiently balancing growth and profitability.

**Customer metrics:** For subscription businesses — Net Revenue Retention (NRR) above 120% means existing customers are spending more each year. A powerful compounding engine.

**TAM and penetration:** How large is the total addressable market? What percentage has the company captured? Companies with 5-15% penetration in a large TAM have the most runway.

## The Best Macro Environment for Growth

Growth stocks dramatically outperform when:
- **Interest rates are falling** — lower discount rates = higher present value of future earnings
- **Economic expansion is early** — companies can grow revenue fast when the macro tide is rising
- **Investor risk appetite is high** — growth requires trusting the future; bull markets build that trust

Growth stocks underperform when rates rise (as 2022 proved dramatically).

## Building a Growth Portfolio

1. **Diversify across 15-25 positions** — no single growth bet should be more than 8-10% of portfolio
2. **Use position sizing based on conviction** — highest conviction = largest positions
3. **Set stop-losses** — growth stocks can fall 40-60% on a bad earnings report
4. **Monitor earnings every quarter** — growth investors buy the growth story; when the story changes, exit

## Key Takeaway

Growth investing works in the right environment and with the right companies. AI strategy helps identify the genuine compounders — high revenue growth, expanding margins, large TAM — and separate them from the story stocks that promise growth but never deliver. Environment matters: pair growth exposure with your macro read on interest rates.
