## What is Technical Analysis?

Technical analysis is the study of price charts and trading data to forecast future price movements. Instead of analyzing a company's financials (fundamental analysis), technical analysts focus on one thing: **price action**.

The core assumption: everything you need to know is already reflected in the price.

## Why Does Technical Analysis Work?

Markets are driven by human emotions — fear and greed. These emotions create recurring patterns. Technical analysis works because human psychology doesn't change. The same patterns that appeared in 1929 appear in 2024 because people react to gains and losses in predictable ways.

It's also self-fulfilling: millions of traders watch the same levels and patterns. When everyone sees the same support level and acts on it, that level becomes support.

## Reading a Basic Chart

**Candlestick chart basics:**
- Each candle represents a time period (1 minute, 1 day, 1 week, etc.)
- **Green/white candle:** Price closed higher than it opened (bullish)
- **Red/black candle:** Price closed lower than it opened (bearish)
- **Wicks (shadows):** Show the high and low prices during the period

**Timeframes:**
- **Long-term investors:** Weekly and monthly charts
- **Swing traders:** Daily and 4-hour charts
- **Day traders:** 5-minute and 15-minute charts

## Key Concepts Every Beginner Needs

**Trend:** The general direction of price movement. "The trend is your friend" — trading in the direction of the trend improves your odds.

**Support:** A price level where buying emerges. Think of it as a floor that price bounces off.

**Resistance:** A price level where selling emerges. Think of it as a ceiling that price struggles to break through.

**Volume:** The number of shares traded. Volume confirms whether a price move is genuine — high volume moves are more meaningful than low volume moves.

**Moving Average:** The average closing price over a set number of periods (e.g., 50-day MA). When price is above a rising moving average, the trend is up. When it crosses below, the trend may be turning.

## The Most Important Chart Pattern: The Breakout

A **breakout** occurs when price moves above a resistance level (or below support) on high volume. Breakouts signal the potential beginning of a new trend and are one of the most traded patterns in markets.

The setup:
1. Price consolidates (moves sideways) for days or weeks
2. Volume contracts (quiet period)
3. Price breaks above resistance on a big volume surge
4. Buy the breakout with a stop below the breakout level

## Key Takeaway

Technical analysis doesn't predict the future — it assesses probabilities. Master support/resistance, trend direction, and volume first. These three concepts alone will make you a more informed trader than most retail investors.
