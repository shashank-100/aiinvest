## One Size Does Not Fit All

The "best stock" is different for every investor. A 65-year-old retiree needs income and capital preservation. A 30-year-old software engineer wants growth. A small business owner needs liquidity. An aggressive trader wants momentum.

The mistake most investors make is copying someone else's portfolio without considering whether it matches their own goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance.

## Defining What "Fits" You

Before screening for stocks, define your investment profile:

**Time horizon:**
- Under 1 year: Capital preservation, dividend income, low volatility
- 1-5 years: Balanced growth and income
- 5-20 years: Growth with some dividend reinvestment
- 20+ years: Maximum growth, tolerate volatility

**Risk tolerance:**
- Conservative: Bonds, dividend blue chips, utilities, consumer staples
- Moderate: Blend of growth and income, index funds + select individual stocks
- Aggressive: Growth stocks, small caps, sector concentrations
- Speculative: Penny stocks, options, early-stage companies

**Income needs:**
- Need regular income: High dividend yield (>3%), REITs, covered call strategies
- No income need: Reinvest everything, pure growth orientation

## Building a Personalized Screen

With your profile defined, the AInvest screener can be configured to return only stocks that match your specific criteria.

**For the income investor:**
- Dividend yield >3%
- Dividend growth rate >5% annually
- Payout ratio &lt;60%
- 10+ year consecutive dividend growth history

**For the growth investor:**
- Revenue growth >25% YoY
- Expanding gross margins
- Positive free cash flow (or clear path to it)
- Large total addressable market

**For the value investor:**
- P/E below sector median
- P/B &lt;2.0
- Return on equity >12%
- Low debt-to-equity

**For the quality investor:**
- ROIC >15%
- Net revenue retention >110% (for SaaS)
- Consistent earnings growth with low variance
- Strong balance sheet (net cash or low debt)

## Evolving With Your Life Stage

Your ideal stocks change as your life changes:
- Early career: Maximize growth, tolerate volatility
- Mid-career: Balance growth with some income
- Pre-retirement: Shift toward dividends and capital preservation
- Retirement: Income focus, minimal drawdown risk

Reviewing your investment profile annually — and updating your screening criteria accordingly — ensures your portfolio remains aligned with your actual needs.

## Key Takeaway

The best stock for you is the one that aligns with your time horizon, risk tolerance, and financial goals — not the stock everyone on social media is talking about. Personalized screening lets you filter the entire market to find your specific opportunities. That's the real power of AI-driven investing.
