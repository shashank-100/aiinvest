## What is Text Type Indicator Search?

AInvest's screener allows you to search for stocks using natural language text queries — describing the conditions you're looking for in plain English rather than manually configuring filter sliders.

This guide explains how to get the most out of text-based indicator searches.

## How It Works

Instead of selecting "P/E ratio less than 15" from a dropdown, you can type: **"undervalued tech stocks with earnings growth"** — and the AI interprets your intent, maps it to the relevant quantitative indicators, and returns matching stocks.

The system understands both plain English and technical terminology.

## Example Queries

**Fundamental queries:**
- "Profitable small cap companies with low debt"
- "Dividend stocks with payout ratio below 60%"
- "Companies with revenue growth above 20% last quarter"
- "Stocks trading below book value with positive free cash flow"

**Technical queries:**
- "Stocks breaking out to 52-week highs on high volume"
- "Oversold stocks with RSI below 30"
- "Stocks above their 200-day moving average with bullish MACD crossover"
- "Low float stocks with unusual volume today"

**Combined queries:**
- "High quality growth stocks at reasonable valuations"
- "Beaten-down value stocks with improving earnings momentum"
- "Defensive dividend stocks with high credit ratings"

## Tips for Better Results

**Be specific about timeframes:** "Revenue growth last quarter" vs "Revenue growth last year" yields different results.

**Combine qualitative and quantitative:** "High quality tech companies with strong balance sheets and positive momentum" works better than just "tech stocks."

**Use industry context:** "Best performing healthcare stocks this month" filters by sector automatically.

**Iterate:** Start broad, then add constraints. "Growth stocks" → "profitable growth stocks" → "profitable growth stocks with analyst upgrades."

## Common Indicators the System Understands

| Term You Type | What It Maps To |
|---|---|
| "Undervalued" | Low P/E, P/B, P/S relative to sector |
| "High quality" | High ROE, low debt, stable earnings |
| "Growth stocks" | Revenue growth >20%, expanding margins |
| "Oversold" | RSI &lt;30, price below lower Bollinger Band |
| "Momentum" | Price performance, relative strength |
| "Value traps" | Cheap P/E but declining revenue |

## Key Takeaway

Text-based search removes the barrier between your investment ideas and actionable stock lists. You don't need to know the exact indicator names — describe what you're looking for and let the AI translate it into data.
