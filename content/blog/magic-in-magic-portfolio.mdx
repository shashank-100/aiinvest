## What is Magic Portfolio?

Magic Portfolio is AInvest's AI-powered portfolio construction system. Rather than giving you individual stock picks, it builds complete, optimized portfolios aligned to specific investment styles — large cap growth, small cap value, dividend income, and more.

Think of it as having a quant hedge fund build your portfolio — without the hedge fund fees.

## How the AI Builds a Portfolio

The Magic Portfolio system runs a multi-factor optimization across thousands of stocks:

**Step 1: Universe Definition**
Define the investable universe based on the portfolio style. A "Large Cap Growth" portfolio starts with S&P 500 companies with market caps >$10B.

**Step 2: Factor Scoring**
Every stock in the universe is scored on:
- **Quality:** ROE, debt levels, earnings stability
- **Momentum:** 3, 6, and 12-month price performance
- **Valuation:** P/E, P/S, EV/EBITDA relative to peers
- **Growth:** Revenue and earnings growth rates

**Step 3: Optimization**
The system selects the top-scoring stocks and weights them to maximize the portfolio's expected Sharpe ratio (return per unit of risk) while maintaining sector diversification.

**Step 4: Risk Controls**
- No single stock exceeds 10% of the portfolio
- Sector concentration limits prevent overexposure
- Correlation analysis ensures diversification is genuine

## Why This Outperforms Manual Stock Picking

**No emotional bias:** The AI applies consistent criteria without fear, greed, or attachment to past winners.

**Factor exposure:** Academic research consistently shows that portfolios with systematic factor exposure (quality, value, momentum) outperform passive indices over full market cycles.

**Rebalancing discipline:** The portfolio is rebalanced systematically, trimming winners and adding to laggards — a disciplined approach most investors fail to execute emotionally.

1. Select the style that matches your investment goals (growth/value/blend × large/mid/small cap)
