## Matching Investments to the Current Environment

There is no single "best investment" — it depends on the economic and market environment. The same stock that's a strong buy in an early-cycle expansion can be a strong sell in late-cycle contraction.

Here's a framework for identifying what to invest in based on where we are.

## The Highest-Conviction Themes Right Now

### Artificial Intelligence Infrastructure
The AI revolution is not hype — it's a multi-decade capital investment cycle. The companies supplying the picks, shovels, and infrastructure for AI build-out are in the early innings:

- **Semiconductors:** GPU demand is structurally elevated. NVDA, AMD are the clear leaders.
- **Cloud computing:** AI workloads migrate to cloud. MSFT Azure, AWS, Google Cloud all benefit.
- **Power and energy infrastructure:** AI data centers require enormous electricity. Utilities and nuclear energy are beneficiaries.
- **Networking:** High-speed networking inside data centers. Arista Networks, Broadcom.

### Healthcare / Biotech
Aging demographics drive structural demand. GLP-1 obesity drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy) represent one of the largest new drug categories in history. The secondary effects — reduced cardiovascular disease, reduced obesity-related conditions — create broad healthcare tailwinds.

### Quality Dividend Growth
In a higher-for-longer rate environment, companies with consistent dividend growth — Dividend Aristocrats — offer competitive income with equity upside. Look for payout ratios below 50% (room to grow the dividend) and earnings growth above inflation.

## Sectors to Be Cautious About

**Commercial Real Estate:** Office vacancy rates remain elevated post-COVID. Regional banks with commercial real estate loan exposure face potential credit losses.

**Consumer Discretionary (low-end):** Lower-income consumer spending is showing stress as savings buffers from the pandemic era deplete and credit card delinquencies rise.

**Speculative Biotech:** Binary drug approval outcomes with no revenue. High risk without the catalyst.
