## Opportunities Exist in Every Market

The bear market of 2022 reset valuations across the board — but it wasn't uniform. Some sectors fell 70-80% (speculative tech, unprofitable growth); others fell 10-20% (energy, healthcare). This non-uniform selloff creates selective opportunities for investors willing to look beyond the headlines.

## Where the Market Has Created Value

### Beaten-Down Quality Tech
The 2022 selloff crushed software and tech valuations indiscriminately. Some genuinely excellent businesses with durable competitive advantages and strong free cash flow fell 50-60% — not because their businesses deteriorated, but because rising rates compressed their multiples.

When rates stabilize, these quality businesses re-rate upward. Screen for: positive FCF, revenue growth >15%, and NTM P/E below the 5-year average.

### Small Cap Value
Small caps underperformed large caps significantly over the past decade. Historically, this divergence eventually reverses. Small cap value stocks — profitable companies trading at low multiples in the small cap universe — have some of the best historical forward return profiles in academic research.

Screen for: market cap $300M-$2B, P/E &lt;15, positive and growing free cash flow, low debt.

### Emerging Market Discounts
Many emerging markets (India, Southeast Asia, Brazil) trade at significant valuation discounts to US markets while growing faster. For investors with a longer time horizon and tolerance for currency risk, selective EM exposure offers compelling risk/reward.

### Contrarian Sector Plays
Every sector has its out-of-favor periods. Today's most hated sectors often become tomorrow's leaders:

- **Biotech:** Post-2021 selloff has reset valuations. Companies with drugs in late-stage trials with large TAMs offer asymmetric upside.
- **Financials:** Regional banks oversold on recession fears — many trade near book value with strong dividend yields.
- **Media and Communication:** Streaming rationalization, ad revenue recovery. Select names at compelling valuations.

## The Setup Matters More Than the Idea

Finding the right sector or theme is only half the work. The other half is timing — buying when the setup is favorable:

- **Oversold conditions:** RSI &lt;30, price below lower Bollinger Band
- **Catalyst visibility:** A specific event in the next 30-90 days (earnings, product launch, Fed decision) that can act as a trigger
- **Institutional accumulation:** Dark pool activity, unusual options interest, increasing analyst coverage
