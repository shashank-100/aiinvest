## Crypto as Part of Your Portfolio

Whether you're a crypto believer or a skeptic, digital assets have become a meaningful part of the investment landscape. Bitcoin, Ethereum, and select altcoins now trade with sufficient liquidity and institutional participation that ignoring them means ignoring a significant portion of investable assets.

For many investors, the question isn't *whether* to have crypto exposure — it's *how much* and *which ones*.

## Why Track Crypto Alongside Stocks?

**Portfolio correlation:** Crypto has historically shown low correlation to stocks during normal markets — making it potentially diversifying. However, during high-stress periods (e.g., 2022), correlation spikes. Tracking both helps you see your true risk exposure.

**Total portfolio view:** If you own NVDA, ETH, and SPY, your total AI/tech exposure includes all three. Seeing them together gives you a clearer picture of your actual thematic concentration.

**Rebalancing:** If crypto rallies 50% while stocks are flat, your allocation has shifted. Tracking both together makes rebalancing decisions obvious.

## Key Crypto Assets and Their Roles

**Bitcoin (BTC)**
The original. Functions as a store of value / digital gold. Increasingly held by institutions and ETFs. The most liquid and least speculative crypto asset.

Portfolio role: Inflation hedge / speculative alternative asset. Most investors size at 1-5% of total portfolio.

**Ethereum (ETH)**
The programmable blockchain. Powers DeFi, NFTs, and smart contracts. More technology exposure than Bitcoin.

Portfolio role: Technology/infrastructure bet on blockchain adoption. Higher volatility than BTC.

**Large-cap altcoins (SOL, AVAX, etc.)**
Higher risk/reward. More speculative. Better treated as small-position, high-conviction bets.

## How Correlation Changes Your Portfolio Risk

A $100K portfolio:
- $80K stocks, $20K crypto → **High correlation to crypto in crypto bull markets**
- $95K stocks, $5K crypto → **Meaningful upside participation with limited downside impact**

The asymmetry is the appeal: crypto's potential for large gains means even a small allocation can meaningfully boost portfolio returns in a crypto bull cycle, while limited downside.

## Key Takeaway

Tracking crypto and stocks together gives you the complete picture of your portfolio risk and allocation. The goal isn't to speculate on crypto — it's to have a systematic, sized exposure that you can monitor and rebalance with the same discipline you apply to your stock portfolio.
