## What are Descriptive Indicators?

When screening penny stocks, **descriptive indicators** are the fundamental statistics that describe a stock's basic characteristics — not its price movement, but its structure. These help you filter for stocks with the right profile before you even look at a chart.

## Key Descriptive Indicators for Penny Stocks

### 1. Market Capitalization
Market cap = share price × shares outstanding.

For penny stocks, look for:
- **Micro-cap:** $50M – $300M
- **Nano-cap:** Under $50M

Smaller caps move faster but carry more risk. Larger micro-caps tend to have more stability and institutional interest.

### 2. Float
**Float** is the number of shares available for public trading (total shares minus insider holdings and restricted shares).

- **Low float (under 10M shares):** Small supply = large price moves on moderate volume. High risk/reward.
- **High float (50M+ shares):** More liquid, harder to move. Better for larger position sizes.

Day traders typically prefer low-float stocks because they can make explosive moves on news.

### 3. Average Daily Volume
Volume tells you how liquid the stock is.

- **Under 100K shares/day:** Dangerous — hard to exit positions, wide spreads
- **100K–500K shares/day:** Tradeable but illiquid
- **500K+ shares/day:** Reasonable liquidity for retail traders

Never trade a penny stock with volume below 100K on a regular day. On a news day, volume can spike 10x — that's when opportunity exists.

### 4. Shares Outstanding vs Float Ratio
A high ratio of total shares to float means insiders hold most shares — potentially a sign of a tight, controlled float. When news hits, limited supply causes rapid price appreciation.

### 5. Short Interest
Short interest shows how many shares are sold short as a percentage of float.

- **High short interest (>20%):** Potential for a short squeeze if the stock moves up
- Low short interest: Less squeeze potential but also less forced selling on way down

## Building a Screening Criteria

A practical penny stock screen:
- Price: $0.50 – $5.00
- Average volume: >200K shares/day
- Float: 1M – 20M shares
- Market cap: $10M – $200M
- Short interest: Check for potential squeeze setups

## Key Takeaway

Descriptive indicators tell you *what kind* of penny stock you're dealing with before you look at price action. A low-float, high-volume stock with news catalyst is fundamentally different from a high-float, low-volume stock. Filter first, then chart.
